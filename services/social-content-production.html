Our founder-led content service helps owners and teams show the human side of the business through short video, photos and honest captions.

People buy from people they feel they know. A short clip of you explaining why you started, or your team laughing through a busy Friday prep, does what no stock graphic can. It gives customers a reason to choose you over the competitor down Federal Highway. That is the heart of our social media marketing Boca Raton work for founder-driven brands.

Why personality is a marketing asset in Boca Raton

Boca Raton is full of excellent businesses. Restaurants near Mizner Park, shops along Palmetto Park Road, service companies in Boca West: the quality bar is high everywhere. What sets one apart is the story. When a customer knows the founder, the values and the crew, price stops being the only factor in the decision.

Founder-led content turns that story into a steady stream of posts. Short origin stories, day-in-the-life clips, lessons learned the hard way, team introductions. None of it needs to be polished like a commercial. It needs to be real, regular and recognizably you. Viewers who watch you for a few weeks feel like they already know you when they walk through the door.

What founder-led content production includes

We plan a content calendar around your story, not generic holidays. Monthly filming captures you talking through real topics: why you opened, what a good day looks like, mistakes that taught you something, how you hire, what your customers never see. Our editors cut that into reels and short clips, pull strong quotes for graphics, and write captions that match how you actually talk.

Team culture gets its own lane. New hire introductions, work anniversaries, behind-the-scenes moments from a packed Saturday. Customers love seeing the people who will serve them. Paired with Community Engagement, these posts also invite comments and conversation, which the platforms reward with more reach.

How we capture your story without eating your week

The process is built for busy owners. One short monthly session, usually at your business, covers most of the filming. We arrive with questions prepared, so you just talk. No scripts to memorize, no teleprompter. If you are camera shy, we start with voiceover, hands-on demonstrations or team features until you find your comfort zone.

Editing is where the personality survives. We cut the pauses, keep the natural phrasing and add captions, because most people watch without sound. You approve everything before it posts. Many owners also add Personal Brand work so their own name carries weight alongside the business name.

Who this fits and what it costs

Founder-led content fits owners who are already the face of the business: restaurant owners, salon owners, contractors, boutique retailers, agency founders. It also fits teams with a strong culture that customers would enjoy seeing. If you would rather stay invisible, this is not the package for you, and we will tell you that honestly before you spend a dollar.

Every service at CROWD Marketing costs $500 per month, month to month, with no long-term contract. That covers planning, filming, editing, graphics, captions and scheduled posting.
