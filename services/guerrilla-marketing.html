CROWD Marketing is a marketing company based at 101 West Camino Real in Boca Raton, Florida. We work with real estate professionals, developers and property teams across Boca Raton, Delray Beach, Boynton Beach, Highland Beach, West Palm Beach and Palm Beach County. Our guerrilla marketing Boca Raton service builds street-level campaigns that make properties and agents unforgettable.

Boca Raton real estate is competitive at every level. Agents compete for listings. Developers compete for buyers. Luxury properties compete for attention from a global audience. Everyone has professional photos, drone footage and a listings page. When every property looks polished online, polish stops being a differentiator. What cuts through is an experience people remember.

Why street-level works for property

Real estate is sold on emotion before logic. Nobody buys a Boca Raton home because of square footage alone. They buy the morning coffee on the balcony, the walk to the beach, the feeling of the neighborhood. A street campaign can deliver that feeling directly.

For agents, visibility is the business. Sellers choose the agent they have heard of. Buyers call the name they recognize. A creative street presence in the neighborhoods you serve builds that recognition faster than any postcard campaign.

For developers and new communities, the challenge is different. You are selling something people cannot walk through yet. A street-level experience near the development site gives potential buyers something tangible: a taste of the lifestyle, a preview of the amenities, a reason to imagine themselves there.

Campaign ideas for agents and developers

For individual agents and teams, we build farm-area campaigns that make you the visible local expert. Branded community events in the neighborhoods you serve. Creative installations near your listings. Street teams at local gatherings with genuinely useful neighborhood content, not just your headshot.

For open houses, we turn a standard showing into an event. Signage that gets photographed. A street presence that draws foot traffic from nearby. An atmosphere that makes the property feel like the place to be that weekend. In a market where buyers see dozens of listings, the one they experienced in person wins.

For developers, we create preview experiences that sell lifestyle before square footage. Pop-up lounges styled like the future amenity spaces. Interactive displays near the site. Launch events that make the development feel like news, not just construction.

The luxury consideration

Boca Raton has a significant luxury market, and luxury requires a different touch. The goal is intrigue, not noise. Exclusivity, not volume.

Luxury campaigns are quieter and more refined. A private preview event with a creative street-level invitation campaign. An artful installation in a high-end shopping district. A concierge-style street presence during season. The audience is smaller, but the value per relationship is enormous, so precision matters more than reach.

We calibrate every campaign to the market segment. What works for a first-time buyer condo does not work for a waterfront estate, and we would never run the same playbook for both.

Connecting street buzz to signed contracts

Attention is only valuable if it converts. Every real estate campaign is designed with a clear path from the street to your CRM.

That means capture mechanics built into the concept: QR codes leading to listing pages, text-to-register for event updates, sign-ups for neighborhood reports. Paired with CRM and lead automation, every interaction becomes a nurtured lead. Paired with personal branding, the agent becomes as memorable as the campaign.

For a full launch of a development or a team, business promotion coordinates the street campaign with wider outreach. See all of our services on the services hub.
